abstract: The homeodomain is one of the most important eukaryotic DNA-binding motifs and has been identified
  in over one thousand proteins. Homeodomain proteins play critical roles in diverse biological processes,
  including cell differentiation and cell pattern formation. The human Pitx2 homeodomain binds several
  different DNA sequences and is a pivotal component of both the TGF-β and Wnt/β-catenin signaling pathways.
  As the recognition of specific DNA sequences represents an essential biochemical function of all DNA-binding
  proteins, we have chosen the Pitx2 homeodomain model to investigate the mechanisms that convey biological
  specificity in these protein-DNA interactions. Here, we report complete chemical shift assignments of
  the human Pitx2 homeodomain and the R24H mutation that induces ring dermoid of the cornea syndrome.
