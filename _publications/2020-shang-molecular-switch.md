abstract: Nuclear receptor (NR) transcription factors use a conserved activation function-2 (AF-2) helix
  12 mechanism for agonist-induced coactivator interaction and NR transcriptional activation. In contrast,
  ligand-induced corepressor-dependent NR repression appears to occur through structurally diverse mechanisms.
  We report two crystal structures of peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma (PPARγ) in an inverse
  agonist/corepressor-bound transcriptionally repressive conformation. Helix 12 is displaced from the
  solvent-exposed active conformation and occupies the orthosteric ligand-binding pocket enabled by a
  conformational change that doubles the pocket volume. Paramagnetic relaxation enhancement (PRE) NMR
  and chemical crosslinking mass spectrometry confirm the repressive helix 12 conformation. PRE NMR also
  defines the mechanism of action of the corepressor-selective inverse agonist T0070907, and reveals that
  apo-helix 12 exchanges between transcriptionally active and repressive conformations-supporting a fundamental
  hypothesis in the NR field that helix 12 exchanges between transcriptionally active and repressive conformations.
