Douglas Kojetin is Professor of Biochemistry and Ingram Professor of Cancer Research at Vanderbilt University. He serves as Director of Graduate Studies in Biochemistry and Co-Leader of the Vanderbilt-Ingram Cancer Center Genome Maintenance Research Program.

His laboratory studies how small molecules regulate nuclear receptor structure, dynamics, and function, combining structural biology, biochemistry, biophysics, chemical biology, and molecular and cellular pharmacology.

He earned a B.S. from Purdue University and a Ph.D. in Biochemistry from North Carolina State University.
